## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an elite, high-precision identity tailored for an AI automation consultancy bridging advanced technology and hard-working regional trade services (plumbing, electrical, HVAC, and security) across Puebla and Cholula. 

The aesthetic is grounded in a **Premium Modern** design movement: it pairs architectural discipline and tight structural lines with high-trust enterprise authority. The visual atmosphere rejects noisy consumer tech tropes in favor of sharp, reliable clarity. It speaks to business owners who demand speed, quantifiable ROI, and operational durability. 

The emotional tone balances institutional solidity with technological precision. Deep navy anchors the visual plane, punctuated by electric blue highlights that signify active automation workflows, while functional green accents provide instant, friction-free conversion paths to messaging platforms. Surfaces feel engineered, exact, and distinctly high-end.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around sharp tonal contrasts that reinforce operational authority and high visual clarity under bright daylight conditions.

- **Primary Deep Navy (`#0B1C3D`)**: The bedrock of the interface. Used for authoritative headline typography, primary dark surfaces, high-contrast framing lines, and high-impact structural containers.
- **Secondary Electric Blue (`#2F6FEB`)**: The active digital signal. Reserved for primary operational CTAs, active component states, interactive highlights, and metric callouts that represent AI infrastructure in motion.
- **Tertiary Conversion Green (`#22C55E`)**: A functional action color strictly dedicated to direct WhatsApp integration channels, verified status indicators, and positive service feedback scores.
- **Neutral Surface Canvas (`#F8F9FF`)**: A crisp, cool-tinted light foundation that elevates white surface cards without creating eye fatigue.
- **Structural Neutral Overlay (`#1E293B`)**: Applied to subheaders and secondary body text for robust contrast ratios exceeding WCAG AAA specifications.

## Typography

The typographical pairing reinforces technical refinement:

- **Display & Headlines (`Manrope`)**: A geometric grotesque with semi-condensed proportions and tight apertures, delivering punchy structural authority in hero moments and section headings.
- **Body & Controls (`Hanken Grotesk`)**: An exacting, unyielding neo-grotesque optimized for rapid scanning on mobile field devices. Provides superior legibility across trade service descriptions, pricing matrices, and automation logs.
- **Micro-Badges & Tags**: Rendered exclusively in `Hanken Grotesk` with `0.08em` tracking and `font-weight: 700` in uppercase, establishing an operational, diagnostic feel.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a responsive fluid grid governed by a strict 4px/8px baseline rhythm:

- **Mobile Viewports (<768px)**: 4-column system with `1rem` (16px) margins and gutters. Elements prioritize single-thumb interaction zones, stacked vertical cards, and high-contrast full-width actions.
- **Tablet Viewports (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters to support dual-column dashboards, automation metric comparisons, and service rosters.
- **Desktop Viewports (>1024px)**: 12-column layout bound to a `75rem` (1200px) maximum container width. Generous `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` section padding isolates discrete value propositions, maintaining clean, uninterrupted visual flow.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages tonal stratification and tight, ambient shadows rather than excessive spatial lift:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#F8F9FF` provides a stable, low-glare surface.
- **Layer 1 (Card Surfaces)**: `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-diffused, cool-tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(11, 28, 61, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(11, 28, 61, 0.02)`. An imperceptible border (`1px solid rgba(11, 28, 61, 0.08)`) maintains structural definition against the background canvas.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Overlays & Menus)**: Elevated components utilize `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(11, 28, 61, 0.12)` with a solid background and hairline border to preserve crisp edges.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Alerts)**: Backed by a high-contrast dark overlay (`rgba(11, 28, 61, 0.75)`) with a subtle `4px` backdrop blur for maximum focus on high-stakes workflow triggers.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a refined, modern industrial feel with `roundedness: 1` (Soft), balancing clean technical geometry with contemporary precision:

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / 4px)**: Form fields, checkboxes, data chips, micro-badges, and secondary tags.
- **Large Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Interactive buttons, alert banners, feature callouts, and segmented controllers.
- **Extra-Large Radius (`0.75rem` / 12px)**: Content cards, conversion panels, testimonial blocks, and core layout groupings.

This controlled curvature prevents components from appearing playful or juvenile, reinforcing the consultancy's identity as an elite, results-focused technical partner.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#2F6FEB` background, pure white text (`label-action`), `0.5rem` radius, with subtle hover elevation (`translateY(-1px)` and shadow expansion).
- **Secondary Action**: Solid `#0B1C3D` background with white text for authoritative enterprise actions.
- **Direct Messaging CTA (WhatsApp)**: Solid `#22C55E` background, high-contrast white text, integrated messaging icon on the leading edge. Mobile variants pinned to full width in sticky bottom trays.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Transparent background with a `1px solid rgba(11, 28, 61, 0.15)` border and `#0B1C3D` text.

### Cards
- Constructed on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a `0.75rem` border radius, hairline `1px solid rgba(11, 28, 61, 0.06)` border, and Layer 1 ambient shadow.
- Inner padding strictly uses `space-lg` (24px) for desktop and `space-md` (16px) for mobile.

### Badges & Status Chips
- Height fixed at `24px` with a `0.25rem` radius.
- Backgrounds use a light tint (e.g., `rgba(47, 111, 235, 0.08)`) with matching solid text in `#2F6FEB`.
- Live automation indicators feature a pulse-dot animation in `#22C55E`.

### Form Controls & Inputs
- Built on a standard `48px` touch target for mobile usability in the field.
- Base styling features a `#FFFFFF` fill, `1.5px solid rgba(11, 28, 61, 0.15)` border, and `0.25rem` radius.
- Focus states invoke a crisp `2px solid #2F6FEB` ring with zero shadow blur for maximum clarity.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Fixed dimensions of `20px x 20px`.
- Checkboxes use `0.25rem` radius; radios are circular.
- Selected state fills with `#2F6FEB` with a solid white check or dot mark.
